# Self-review of synthesized B2–C2 items (2026-10-21)
All items are needs-review. Every token appears verbatim in an attested source (see token_evidence), but attestation is per token, not per sentence, so grammaticality and tone-in-context are NOT proven. Rejected drafts are listed in package_log.json. Each language has fewer than 30 items, so every item was reviewed.

## sw (31: 17 synthesized + 14 attested proverbs)
- Relatives (nilichonunua, aliyekuja), passive/reciprocal/causative (andikwa, pendana, shangaza), habitual hu-, Hata hivyo, Licha ya, Kwa upande mmoja: standard and natural.
- "Shikamoo, mzee": correct register. The culture note (Marahaba reply) is drawn from general knowledge, so a reviewer must confirm it.
- The proverbs are attested in news text, but their glosses are mine. "Haraka haraka haina baraka" is only attested in Wikipedia text (cross-check).
- Watch: agglutinated forms are often unattested, so drafts drift toward simpler forms (the -nge-, applicative and -ta- negative drafts were rejected). Also check English-calque word order in argumentative passages.

## yo (13: synthesized only)
- Serial verbs (mú…wá, gbé…lọ), focus ni, Bí…bá, ó yẹ kí, the ideophone báláú, and ẹ kú iṣẹ́ are standard constructions.
- Risk: each toned token is attested somewhere, but possibly for a homograph (e.g. rí/ri, lọ/lọ̀). Tone in context is unverified, and elision forms (lánàá) vary.
- No toned proverb is attested. "Ọwọ́ ọmọdé kò tó pẹpẹ…" is found only untoned, so it is a candidate.
- Watch: tone homographs, missing low-tone marks on pronouns, the dot-below vs plain ẹ/ọ distinction.

## ha (11: 9 synthesized + 2 proverbs)
- Grammar is sound: relative da, counterfactual da…da, habitual kan, Ko da yake, Sannu da zuwa.
- Main defect: the corpus writes plain k/d/b, so kofa, bunkasa and bukatar are attested but non-standard. A reviewer must restore ƙ/ɗ/ɓ (ƙofa, bunƙasa, buƙatar). Tone and vowel length are withheld by design.
- Watch: hooked letters, grammatical gender agreement (ta/ya), and the possessive linker -n/-r.

## ig (9: 8 synthesized + 1 proverb)
- Biko, Otú ọ dị, Mgbe…, O doro anya na: fine. Tone marks are absent (normal written practice), so tone_unverified applies.
- Suspect: "O were akwụkwọ ahụ bịa" (more idiomatic: "O wetara akwụkwọ ahụ"), and "mmiri ezo" (likely needs "mmiri zoo"). Both are flagged for a reviewer.
- Watch: subjunctive/conditional verb forms, the dotted vowels ị ọ ụ ṅ, and vowel harmony on prefixes.

## zu (13: 10 synthesized + 3 proverbs)
- Applicative phekela, passive yabhalwa, reciprocal -an-, relative engiyibone (OC -yi- for class 9 indoda), ngicela + subjunctive, Noma kunjalo, Kusobala ukuthi: standard.
- "Izandla ziyagezana" and "Inkunzi isematholeni" are attested only as titles (a programme and a book), so the proverb sense is a cross-check claim.
- Watch: concord across noun classes, the remote/recent past (wa- vs -e), and the hlonipha lexicon.

## xh (11: 9 synthesized + 2 proverbs)
- Mirrors zu with Xhosa forms: ndicela undincede, Nangona kunjalo, ucango luvuliwe (class 11 concord lu-), ibhalwe ngumlawuli.
- Watch: Zulu interference (ukudla vs ukutya was handled), the ii-/izi- class 10 forms, and the copulative ngu-/yi-.
