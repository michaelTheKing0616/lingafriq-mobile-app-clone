#### **1. Language Accuracy** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

**Polie Strengths:**
- ✅ **Diacritics Enforcement**: Automatic correction for tonal languages (Yoruba, Igbo, Twi, Wolof)
- ✅ **12 African Languages**: More comprehensive than most competitors
- ✅ **Fuzzy Matching**: Handles typos and variations intelligently
- ✅ **Phrase-Level Correction**: Context-aware corrections, not just word-level
- ✅ **Cultural Accuracy**: Respects formal/informal registers, honorifics, social hierarchy

**vs. Competitors:**
- **Duolingo**: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) - Good for major languages, weak on African languages, no diacritics enforcement
- **Babbel**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) - Better accuracy but limited African language support

**VERDICT**: Polie **SURPASSES** all competitors in African language accuracy.
