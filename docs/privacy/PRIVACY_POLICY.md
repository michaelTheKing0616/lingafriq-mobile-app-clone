# LingAfriq privacy policy

This page says what we collect and why. It says how long we keep data and who can see it. It matches our data map in `RETENTION.yaml` (version 1).

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Effective:** 27 October 2026

## Account, learner profile, or child-safety policy

**What we collect:** Email or sign-in info, your name in the app, birth year only, language picks, a guardian contact when the law says we must, and safety settings like child mode.

**Why:** To run your account, sign you in, follow child-safety rules, and hide features that are not right for your age.

**How long:** Until you delete your account, or sooner when a guardian asks us to remove a child account.

**Who sees it:** LingAfriq staff for support and safety; hosting partners under contract; not sold to ads.

## Curriculum, product configuration, or reference catalogue

**What we collect:** Lesson packs, word lists, and app settings on your device or our servers. This is mostly content, not facts about you.

**Why:** To show the right lessons, languages, and offline packs.

**How long:** Content follows our release plan; your download choices clear when you remove the app or reset packs.

**Who sees it:** You; our content team for fixes; hosting partners under contract.

## Learning progress and gamification state

**What we collect:** Lesson scores, streaks, cowries, paths you finish, review history, and passport progress tied to your account.

**Why:** To set fair difficulty, remember what you learned, and show rewards.

**How long:** Until you delete your account unless law needs a shorter window.

**Who sees it:** You; systems that run the app; support staff if you ask for help.

## Security, reliability, and product analytics

**What we collect:** Device type, crash logs, coarse usage counts, and security events. We do not need your lesson answers for this.

**Why:** To stop abuse, fix bugs, and see if features work.

**How long:** Most security logs roll off within about one year. Crash reports go sooner when fixed.

**Who sees it:** Our tech and trust teams; analytics vendors under contract if you leave analytics on in settings.

## Social and messaging features

**What we collect:** Friends you add, messages in Village or chat, tribe or class membership, and reports you file.

**Why:** To let you learn with others safely.

**How long:** Until you delete content, leave a space, or delete your account. Some safety reports stay longer for review.

**Who sees it:** People you choose to share with; moderators for safety; police only when the law requires.

## Speech practice, scoring, or licensed voice assets

**What we collect:** Short audio when you use speaking drills, Polie voice chat, Lens, or speech scoring. We also store text from those turns. You may send optional clips to help the dictionary.

**Why:** To score your speech, give feedback, and improve licensed voices when you opt in.

**How long:** By default we **delete raw audio after scoring**. If you turn on “save practice recordings,” we keep clips up to one year. We delete within 30 days after you turn it off. Model-training help is separate. It stays off unless you opt in.

**Who sees it:** Scorers on our servers; human reviewers for safety or quality queues you start; never public without your consent.

## Children, teens, and guardians

If you are under the age that needs a parent in your country, we ask a guardian before we store account data. **Child mode** limits social chat, shop, ads, and open persona chat. Teens stay private. Adults they do not know cannot send them direct messages. Guardians can ask us to delete a child account. **Minors are not offered** the separate model-training opt-in.

## Your choices

You can export or delete your data from **Settings → Privacy → Your data**. You can turn off analytics, voice storage, and training help in Privacy settings. Email privacy@lingafriq.com with questions.

## Words we use (plain meanings)

| Term | Meaning in this app |
| --- | --- |
| Personal data | Info that can identify you or your learning account. |
| Legal basis — contract | We need the data to provide the service you signed up for. |
| Legal basis — legitimate interest | We use data in ways you expect (security, fixing bugs) without overriding your rights. |
| Processor | A company that hosts or runs tools for us under our rules. |
| Controller | LingAfriq decides why and how your data is used. |
| Opt-in | You turn a feature on; we do not assume consent. |

## Changes

When we update this policy we bump the version and date at the top. Big changes ask you to read and tap **I understand** in the app before you continue.
