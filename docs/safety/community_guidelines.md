# Community guidelines

We built LingAfriq so learners can practice African languages together. These rules keep Village, chat, and classrooms safe and kind.

## Rule 1 · Be kind {#clause-kind}

Do not bully, threaten, or pile on someone who is learning.

**Example:** Do not mock a learner because they spelled ọkọ̀ wrong in a circle chat.

## Rule 2 · No hate {#clause-hate}

Do not attack people for who they are, where they are from, or what they believe.

**Example:** Do not use slurs about tribe, faith, or nationality in messages or snaps.

## Rule 3 · No spam {#clause-spam}

Do not flood chats with ads, scams, or the same link over and over.

**Example:** Do not paste a crypto promo in every classroom thread.

## Rule 4 · Help others learn {#clause-learning}

Share tips that match our lessons. Do not derail practice with noise.

**Example:** When someone asks about tone marks, point them to the lesson card instead of guessing.

## Rule 5 · Keep everyone safe {#clause-safety}

Do not share violent, sexual, or self-harm content. Report anything that feels unsafe.

**Example:** Do not post adult images in Village clips meant for language practice.

## Rule 6 · Play fair {#clause-respect}

Use your real learner profile. Do not pretend to be staff or another person.

**Example:** Do not claim you are a moderator to scare someone into sending money.
