## Recipe
- **Friends tab:** search + "Invite on WhatsApp" (a prefilled link with a referral code, shared through the OS share sheet), QR code to add someone nearby, and contacts matching only after asking.
- **Friend streak (Finch-style):** a shared flame on both avatars and "You and Tunde: 12 days". A gentle nudge button (preset messages only).
- **Circles (Discord-style):** a group per language or family (up to 50), with a pinned weekly challenge, a chat, and a voice room ("Practice room: 3 speaking"). Each circle has a host.
- **Chat:** WhatsApp-like bubbles and hold-to-record voice notes with a waveform. Voice notes can be marked "Please correct my pronunciation".
- **Leagues (08, 09):** weekly league of 30 with friends pinned, and promotion/demotion zones in soft colours.
- **Safety (under 18, from the pack 1 age data):** no open DMs. Chat with approved friends only, or preset phrases. Circles need a parent's OK. Report/block on every message (long-press). Contact info and links are auto-filtered. A human review queue. Voice rooms aren't available to minors.
Motion: a new voice note slides in at 200 ms. A nudge sends a little Polie wave across the screen (500 ms).
Upgrade for LingAfriq: family circles ("Grandma's Igbo circle") where elders can send voice notes for learners to copy.
