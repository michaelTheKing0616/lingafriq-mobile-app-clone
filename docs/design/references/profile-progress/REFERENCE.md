## Recipe
1. **Header:** a 96 px avatar (Polie-themed frame), name, "Learning since Mar 2026", flags of the languages learned. Gear (settings) on the right and share on the left, 44 px each.
2. **Stats grid (2×2, 01):** streak, total XP, league, words known, each with an icon, a big number and a small label.
3. **Streak calendar (02):** a month grid where done days are filled gold, frozen days get a blue snowflake, and today has a ring. Swipe between months.
4. **Languages:** a row per language with its flag, a level ("Beginner 2 of 6", 06) and a thin progress bar.
5. **League (04):** a card showing "Gold League · #4", plus a top-3 mini podium. Your row is pinned and highlighted.
6. **Badges (05):** a 3-column grid. Locked badges are grey outlines with a "2 of 5" ring. Tap one to see how it's earned.
7. **Share card (07):** a 9:16 card (stats + Polie + language name in its own script), shared through the OS share sheet, with a small "made with LingAfriq" line.
Motion: numbers count up once per open (600 ms). Unlocking a badge flips it (400 ms) with a shine. Reduce Motion swaps for fades.
Upgrades for LingAfriq: an "Offline days count too" note. A share image under 200 KB (WhatsApp-friendly). Under-18 profiles are private by default (NDPA/COPPA, from pack 1), with no public leaderboard name.
