## Recipe
1. **Duel lobby:** two avatar cards ("You vs ?"), a language and level chip, buttons for "Find a match", "Challenge a friend" and "Practise vs Polie".
2. **Matchmaking:** avatars spin through faces for 1–5 s, then the opponent lands with a bounce (400 ms spring). After 10 s, offer "Play Polie instead".
3. **Round UI (Kahoot-style):** a top bar with both scores, the round dots (1 of 5) and a 10 s timer bar (turns coral in the last 3 s, with tick haptics). The question is in 24 px bold with audio. 4 answer tiles, each with a shape and colour (not colour alone). 3 lives shown as hearts.
4. **Reveal:** the right tile pulses green and the wrong one shakes. Points fly to the score (400 ms).
5. **Win/lose:** a full-screen result with Polie (dance on a win, a hug on a loss: "So close!"), the XP earned and a chest. Buttons: Rematch (main), "Play someone new", "Review the words". A loss never costs anything you paid for.
6. **Practice games (07, 08):** swipe matching, a speed round, a listening game. Fully offline.
7. **Offline:** duels need a connection, so with no network, show "Play Polie offline" and queue XP to sync later.
Fairness: match by level, report players, and for under-18s no chat in duels (preset emotes only).
Upgrade for LingAfriq: an "Ayo/Ludo-style" tournament week with family brackets, and proverb rounds (finish the proverb).
