# 1. DUOLINGO

**Category:** Gamified Freemium Language + Education Platform  
**Model:** Freemium (Free / Super Duolingo / Duolingo Max)  
**Active Users:** 130M+ monthly active (as of 2025)  
**Paying Subscribers:** 10.9M (June 2025)

---

## 1.1 Language Coverage

- **40+ languages available** for English speakers, including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese (Mandarin), Korean, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Irish, Turkish, Greek, Hindi, Arabic, Hebrew, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Swahili, Zulu, Romanian, Hungarian, Finnish, Czech, Welsh, Ukrainian, Catalan (for Spanish speakers), Cantonese (for Mandarin speakers)
- **Fictional and constructed languages:** High Valyrian (Game of Thrones), Klingon (Star Trek), Esperanto
- **English courses for speakers of 26 other languages** (e.g., English for Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish speakers)
- **148 new courses launched April 2025** using AI-generated content, making Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Mandarin available in all 28 supported UI languages
- **CEFR alignment:** Top 7 courses redesigned from scratch to align with A1–B2 CEFR levels; coverage extends toward C1 in flagship languages
- Courses with more learners receive more content, features, and AI-enhanced exercises

---

## 1.2 Core Lesson Engine

- **Linear progression path:** Single scrollable map with nodes representing lessons (post-2022 redesign — the old branching tree was retired)
- **Lesson types available:**
  - Multiple choice (select the correct translation)
  - Tap the words in order (arrange words to form a sentence)
  - Type what you hear (transcription exercise)
  - Speak the phrase (voice recognition exercise)
  - Match pairs (vocabulary matching game — "Match Madness")
  - Fill-in-the-blank (complete a sentence)
  - Translate from native to target language
  - Translate from target to native language
  - Select the image that matches the word
  - Read a sentence aloud and get scored
  - Listen and select (hear audio, choose correct written form)
- **Lesson length:** Typically 3–5 minutes, designed as "bite-sized" daily habits
- **Hearts system (free users):** 5 hearts; each wrong answer costs a heart; run out = lesson locked until regeneration or purchase. Hearts regenerate over time.
- **Energy system (replaced hearts on some platforms):** Functions similarly, limits daily lesson count for free users
- **Mistake review:** After completing a lesson, errors are flagged and offered as immediate review
- **Skill tips:** Some lessons include expandable grammar explanations (accessed via a lightbulb icon before the lesson starts)
- **Section checkpoints:** After completing a set of skills, a checkpoint test evaluates readiness to advance
- **Legendary lessons:** Complete all levels of a skill to unlock a "Legendary" purple crown — harder exercises with no mistakes allowed

---

## 1.3 Adaptive Learning & Personalization

- **AI-driven difficulty adaptation:** Lessons adjust in real-time based on performance; consistently aced exercises lead to harder variants
- **Spaced repetition system (SRS):** Words introduced, then reintroduced at spaced intervals across future lessons
- **Personalized practice:** A dedicated "Practice" section surfaces words and grammar based on your error history
- **Placement test:** Skip beginner content by taking an onboarding placement test
- **A/B testing at scale:** Every feature — lesson types, notification copy, push schedules — is A/B tested across millions of users before global rollout
- **Streak calendar:** Shows consecutive days of practice; completing any lesson counts
- **Streak freeze:** Purchase or earn a freeze that protects a streak if you miss one day (Super users get automatic protection)

---

## 1.4 Gamification Features

- **XP (Experience Points):** Earned per lesson; used to rank in leagues and count toward goals
- **Daily XP goals:** User-selectable targets (Casual = 10 XP/day, Regular = 20, Serious = 30, Intense = 50)
- **Leagues:** Weekly competitive ranking with 30 randomly selected users. Tiers: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Sapphire → Ruby → Emerald → Amethyst → Pearl → Obsidian → Diamond. Top performers promote to next league; bottom are relegated.
- **Gems/Lingots (virtual currency):** Earned from lessons and streaks; spent in the shop on streak freezes, heart refills, bonus lessons, cosmetic items, timed practice
- **Achievements/Badges:** Unlocked from objectives (e.g., "Complete 10 lessons," "7-day streak," "Earn 100 XP in a week")
- **Monthly challenges:** Themed challenges for additional XP and special badges
- **Daily quests:** 3 rotating quests per day; completing all 3 grants bonus rewards (boosted in 2025)
- **Friends quest:** Collaborative challenge with friends toward shared goals (matching system added for users without friends)
- **Seasonal app icons:** Cosmetic icons to change the app icon (Halloween, holidays, etc.)
- **Character-driven lessons:** Story characters (Lily, Eddy, Oscar, Bea, etc.) appear in exercises and Stories to provide narrative context
- **"Hard mode" after perfect lessons:** Introduced as "Eddy's Workout" — more challenging exercises unlocked when you nail a perfect lesson

---

## 1.5 Stories Feature

- **Interactive short stories** with dialogue between characters
- Available in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and 100+ additional courses (expanded 2024–2025)
- Exercises embedded mid-story: multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, translation
- Expressive character voices and animated reactions
- Difficulty scales from beginner to intermediate
- Stories earn XP and count toward daily goals
- Can be completed offline (cached content)

---

## 1.6 DuoRadio (Audio Feature)

- Radio-style listening comprehension episodes
- Initially English + target language bilingual; gradually transitions to monolingual (immersive)
- Beginner episodes added to 220+ courses in 2025
- Covers all top 9 learning languages at all levels in 2025
- Episodes on topics ranging from daily life to news-adjacent content
- Can be played in background / while multitasking

---

## 1.7 Video Call with Lily (Max Feature)

- **AI-powered simulated video call** with Lily, Duolingo's AI character
- Real-time conversational practice in target language
- Available to **Duolingo Max subscribers** for English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean (expanded to 9 languages 2025, up from 3 in 2024)
- Onboarding support to ease anxiety around speaking
- Real-time feedback on grammar and vocabulary during the call
- Post-call review: breakdown of performance with suggestions
- Lily adapts difficulty to learner's level (beginner-friendly mode)
- Lily's responses are dynamic and personalized (upgraded in 2025)
- Available on Android and iOS

---

## 1.8 Adventures (Exploration Feature)

- Interactive scenario-based learning launched at Duocon 2024
- Immerses learner in the Duolingo character world
- Navigation scenarios: ordering food, asking directions, checking into hotels
- More game-like exploration than standard lessons
- Gradually rolling out across courses

---

## 1.9 Flashcard Exercises

- Added for top 8 learning languages (2025)
- Vocabulary reinforcement through spaced flashcard presentation
- Train memory through recall rather than recognition
- Complements story and lesson content

---

## 1.10 Duolingo Score (Proficiency Credential)

- Numerical scale 0–160 representing language proficiency
- Based on cumulative lesson performance and testing
- **LinkedIn integration launched September 2025** — first-of-its-kind ability to add a language learning score directly to a LinkedIn profile
- Bridges informal learning with real-world credential signaling

---

## 1.11 Social Features

- **Follow friends:** View friends' streaks and XP on a friends leaderboard
- **In-app news feed:** Highlights learner achievements, makes sharing wins easier (upgraded 2025)
- **Friends quests:** Collaborative daily challenges done together
- **Universal matching system:** Even users without friends can join Friends Quests
- **Profiles:** Public-facing profile with streak, XP, leagues, languages studied, level

---

## 1.12 Duolingo English Test (DET)

- Fully separate certified English proficiency test
- Accepted by 5,000+ institutions (universities, employers) worldwide
- 1-hour test taken at home
- Results in 48 hours
- Measures Literacy, Comprehension, Conversation, and Production
- Costs around $59 (significantly cheaper than IELTS/TOEFL)
- Score report includes video interview and writing sample

---

## 1.13 Beyond Languages: Math, Music, Chess

- **Duolingo Math:** Covers elementary through grades 6–9 (algebra, geometry, statistics). 60 new units added 2025. Navigation allows free movement between units.
- **Duolingo Music:** Piano and sheet music lessons using a Guitar Hero-style interface. Song partnerships (Sony Music for popular tracks). Partnership with Loog for a physical companion digital piano ($249). Available in dozens of UI languages.
- **Duolingo Chess:** Launched June 2025 on iOS, October 2025 on Android. Bite-sized puzzles, coaching from character "Oscar." PvP mode (player vs player live matches) launched in late 2025. Mini-match games for endgame practice.
- **Duolingo ABC:** Separate children's app for early literacy (letters, sounds, phonics)

---

## 1.14 Duolingo for Schools (Educator Tool)

- **Classroom dashboard** for teachers
- Assign lessons to students
- Track individual and class XP, streak, completion rates
- Set weekly XP goals per student
- Roster management
- Free for schools and classrooms

# 2. BABBEL

**Category:** Structured Curriculum Language Learning  
**Model:** Paid Subscription (Free trial only, no permanent free tier)  
**Languages:** 14 (Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish, Dutch, Indonesian, English)

---

## 2.1 Core Curriculum Structure

- **Expert-designed lessons** by a team of 200+ professional linguists and language teachers
- **CEFR levels A1 through C1** depending on the language (German, Spanish, French go deepest)
- Lessons are **not locked** — learners can jump to any lesson or course regardless of completion status
- **Lesson length:** 10–15 minutes per lesson (some Spanish lessons even shorter)
- **Lesson structure:** Each lesson focuses on a single theme or grammar concept through a sequence of exercises
- **Courses are topical**, not just grammatical: travel, food, business, culture, dialects, idioms, slang
- Over **60,000 individual lessons** across all 14 languages
- **Business-specific courses** covering: writing professional emails, networking vocabulary, delivering presentations, client calls, industry jargon (healthcare, hospitality, IT, finance, construction)
- Tailored from the learner's native language — a German learning Spanish sees different grammar notes than an English speaker learning Spanish

---

## 2.2 Exercise Types

- **Dialogue exercises:** Listen to and participate in real conversations
- **Fill-in-the-blank grammar:** Complete sentences with correct conjugations or vocabulary
- **Multiple choice vocabulary:** Select correct translation or form
- **Image matching:** Connect vocabulary to visuals
- **Pronunciation exercises (with speech recognition):** Speak into the microphone, get scored
- **Writing exercises:** Type out words or sentences in the target language
- **Listen and select:** Hear a word or phrase, choose the matching written form
- **Reorder words:** Arrange shuffled words into correct sentence order
- **True or False:** Evaluate whether a statement is correct
- **Speak aloud:** Read prompts aloud and receive pronunciation scoring

---

## 2.3 Speech Recognition & Pronunciation

- **AI-Enhanced Speech Recognition** trained on millions of data points from Babbel's audio library
- System evaluates pronunciation accounting for **different accents and dialects** (not just one "perfect" accent)
- Gives **personalized feedback** on specific sounds to correct
- Human voice recordings used for reference audio — not synthetic TTS
- Real-life dialogues recorded by native speakers in authentic environments

---

## 2.4 Babbel Speak (AI Feature — Launched September 2025)

- New AI-powered **voice-led conversation trainer**, added as a separate tab on the home screen
- Specifically designed for **first-time speakers** — addresses the gap between knowing words and actually using them
- Real-life scenario dialogues: ordering coffee in Paris, greeting a neighbor in German, shopping in Italy
- Pedagogically curated scenarios by language experts; AI handles natural conversational flow
- Judgment-free environment — designed to reduce anxiety
- Scaffolded approach: guided at first, gradually increases learner autonomy
- Provides personalized responses in real-time (NLP-powered)
- Feedback after each session: new phrases learned and areas for improvement
- Available in Spanish, French, German, Italian initially

---

## 2.5 Review Manager

- **Spaced repetition review system** tracks vocabulary and grammar items
- Users can choose review mode: writing, speaking, listening, or flashcards
- Personalizes what gets reviewed based on error history
- Works offline once content is cached
- Can review across all courses or filter by topic/lesson

---

## 2.6 Podcasts (Babbel Podcasts)

- Audio content to **learn on the go** — commuting, exercising, walking
- Covers real-life topics in an accessible format
- Language-learner-focused (not native-speaker podcasts)
- Available for some languages (Spanish, French, German most complete)

---

## 2.7 Live Classes (Babbel Live)

- **Instructor-led online group classes** with certified teachers
- Available as 1-on-1 sessions or group sessions (4–6 learners per class)
- Focused on conversational practice and real-time feedback
- Topics organized by skill level and theme
- Scheduled at flexible times; can book ahead
- Add-on purchase or included in premium business plans
- Classes conducted on the Babbel platform (no Zoom required)
- Classes available for Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, English

---

## 2.8 Placement Test

- Available at account setup or any time
- Assesses current vocabulary and grammar knowledge
- Places learner at appropriate CEFR level
- Allows experienced learners to skip foundational content

---

## 2.9 Progress & Motivation System

- **Activity streaks** (daily practice streak)
- **Achievement badges** for completing courses, maintaining streaks, lesson counts
- **Progress dashboard:** Lessons completed, words learned, time spent
- Tracks learning across CEFR levels to show advancement
- No gamification league/competition elements (deliberately un-gamified compared to Duolingo)

---

## 2.10 Cultural Insights

- Grammar tips and cultural context **embedded throughout lessons** rather than in a separate section
- Region-specific courses available (e.g., differences between Spanish in Spain vs Latin America)
- Dialect courses (e.g., German dialects, regional French accents)

---

## 2.11 Babbel for Business

- **Admin control panel** for managers: track employee learning progress, usage statistics, completion rates, time spent
- Assign specific courses to specific employees
- User management: add/remove learners, manage access permissions
- Annual business reviews with a dedicated Customer Success Manager
- B2B pricing per seat
- Can integrate with internal HR platforms
- Business-specific course library separate from consumer content
- Inter Miami CF (MLS soccer team, 15+ nationalities) uses Babbel as a named enterprise customer

---

## 2.12 Platforms & Offline

- iOS, Android, Web (no special software for web — browser-based)
- **Offline access:** Download lessons for offline use on mobile
- Seamlessly syncs progress across all devices

---

# 3. ELSA SPEAK

**Category:** AI-Powered English Pronunciation & Speech Coach  
**Model:** Freemium  
**Focus:** English pronunciation, fluency, accent reduction, and professional communication  
**Languages supported for learners:** 50+ native languages (app delivers feedback in learner's language)

---

## 3.1 Initial Assessment

- **16-sentence spoken assessment** at onboarding
- Produces: overall fluency level (Beginner / Elementary / Intermediate / Upper Intermediate / Advanced), overall score, pronunciation scores for each individual phoneme/letter, **predicted IELTS score** based on speech patterns
- Identifies specific sounds the learner struggles with
- Assessment can be retaken at any point to track progress
- Results are the foundation of the personalized learning path

---

## 3.2 Core Pronunciation Engine

- **Proprietary AI speech recognition** developed specifically for non-native English speakers (not a repurposed voice assistant)
- Trained on voice data from people speaking English with **dozens of different accents**, so it accurately recognizes non-native speech patterns (unlike Siri or Google Assistant)
- Backed by **Google's Gradient Ventures** (AI-focused VC arm)
- Provides **phoneme-level feedback** — breaks down which specific sounds within a word were wrong
- **Color-coded feedback:** Green = correct, Red = incorrect, with visual phoneme indicators
- Scores: individual phonemes, word stress, sentence intonation, overall fluency
- Word stress analysis: detects whether emphasis falls on the correct syllable
- Sentence intonation analysis: rise/fall pitch patterns that convey meaning and emotion
- **27 hours of ELSA study ≈ one ESL speaking course at an American university** (company claim)

---

## 3.3 Lesson Library

- **8,000+ lessons** across topics
- Categories: daily conversation, business English, academic English, travel, healthcare, technology, social situations
- **Career-specific lesson packs:** Lessons tailored to profession (doctors, engineers, customer service, sales)
- **Exam preparation:** IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC, EIKEN, Pearson PTE, U.S. Citizenship test, Oxford proficiency — dedicated structured prep courses for each
- Lessons cover: individual sounds, words, phrases, sentences, full conversations
- Each lesson includes: listen to model audio → practice speaking → get scored → review feedback → retry
- Idioms and common expressions (practical, not just textbook)

---

## 3.4 Personalized Study Plans

- AI-generated daily study plan based on:
  - Initial assessment results
  - Job role and professional context
  - Learner's interests
  - Skill gaps identified over time
- Plan adapts daily based on performance
- **Native language support:** Explanations and tips delivered in learner's native language (Hindi, Indonesian, Spanish, Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese, Thai, Portuguese, etc.)
- Targets weak phonemes specifically in each session
- Sets daily practice time goals (10 minutes/day recommended)

---

## 3.5 ELSA AI (Generative AI Conversational Tutor)

- **ELSA AI launched globally 2023**, expanded to full generative AI capabilities by 2025
- Voice-based AI conversation partner that **responds in real-time like a human tutor**
- Learner speaks → ELSA responds naturally → provides feedback on grammar, fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary simultaneously
- Supports open-ended dialogue (not just scripted scenarios)
- Creates custom practice scenarios on request
- Real-time corrections mid-conversation
- Post-conversation detailed report: what went well, what to improve, key phrases to review
- Described by users as "feeling like talking to a real person"

---

## 3.6 Role-Play Scenarios

- Pre-built interactive role-plays simulating real-life situations
- Examples: job interview, business presentation, team meeting, client phone call, conference introduction
- Learner plays one side of the dialogue; ELSA AI plays the other
- Scenario-specific vocabulary and phrases pre-loaded
- Scored on: pronunciation, fluency, vocabulary appropriateness, grammar, intonation

---

## 3.7 Speech Analyzer Tool

- Submit **any spoken text** (not just lesson content) for analysis
- Record yourself reading an article, script, or free speech
- Receive a full report: pronunciation score, fluency score, intonation map, word stress chart, specific sounds needing improvement
- Share analysis results with a teacher or coach
- Used by corporate clients for employee assessment

---

## 3.8 Dictionary Feature

- Built-in **spoken dictionary** — look up any word
- Hear native pronunciation
- Practice saying the word and get scored immediately
- Available to free users (one of the few free features)
- Contextual example sentences

---

## 3.9 Interactive Games

- **Missing Letters:** Vocabulary reinforcement with letter completion
- **Word Shuffle:** Rearrange scrambled letters to form words
- More games to reinforce pronunciation practice in an engaging way
- Helps break up the intensity of core pronunciation drills

---

## 3.10 Tracking & Analytics

- **Detailed performance dashboard** showing:
  - Pronunciation score per phoneme over time
  - Fluency trend chart
  - Word stress accuracy
  - Intonation accuracy
  - Lessons completed per day
  - Streak
  - Phonemes mastered vs. still needing work
- **Predicted IELTS band score** updated as you improve
- Visual progress maps showing improvement over weeks/months

---

## 3.11 ELSA for Business & Education

- **Enterprise dashboard:** Managers can assign tests, track team-level progress, see usage data
- **ELSA Testing Platform:** Create and assign custom assessments for employees/students; test by topic or skill type
- Classroom version for schools and universities
- API available for integration into third-party platforms
- Reduces corporate training costs (claimed 25–30x cost reduction vs. offline training courses)

# 4. TALKPAL

**Category:** AI Conversational Language Tutor  
**Model:** Freemium (Free 5–10 min/day / Premium Unlimited)  
**Languages:** 57 languages  
**Technology:** GPT-powered AI

---

## 4.1 Core Learning Modes

TalkPal's defining feature is its breadth of interactive practice modes:

**Chat Mode**
- Open-ended back-and-forth text conversation with an AI tutor on any topic
- AI adjusts complexity to learner's proficiency level
- Immediate grammar and vocabulary corrections after each message
- Learner can choose any topic or let AI suggest

**Call Mode**
- **Audio-only phone call** simulation with an AI tutor
- No screen required — learner speaks, AI responds verbally
- Simulates the anxiety-inducing real-world experience of a phone call in a foreign language
- Corrections delivered audibly after the exchange

**Roleplay Mode**
- Real-world scenario simulations: ordering at a restaurant, booking a hotel, grocery shopping, job interview, airport navigation
- Learner plays their role; AI plays the other character
- Failure to use correct language triggers gentle correction and retry
- 300+ unique scenario environments

**Character Mode**
- Chat with **historical and fictional characters** in the target language
- Examples: have a conversation with Shakespeare in English, Einstein in German, Napoleon in French, Cleopatra in Arabic
- Educational + engaging; characters stay in persona
- Historical context woven into the conversation

**Debate Mode**
- AI assigns a **controversial position** and learner must defend it in the target language
- Designed for advanced learners building argumentative vocabulary
- AI challenges the learner's points to force deeper language use
- Trains formal register and complex grammar structures

**Photo Mode**
- Upload or generate an image; **describe what you see** in the target language
- AI evaluates the description, asks follow-up questions
- Upload a photo from your camera roll (e.g., your room, a meal) and describe it
- Trains descriptive vocabulary in authentic personal context

**Sentence Mode**
- Listen to a sentence; **repeat it** in the target language
- Designed for complete beginners building basic sentence patterns
- AI checks pronunciation and sentence accuracy

**Word Mode**
- Practice individual **words in context** with pronunciation feedback
- Hear, repeat, see the word used in sentences

**Dialogue Mode**
- Guided **role-play dialogues** for specific daily situations
- More structured than free roleplay — follows a script with flexibility

---

## 4.2 AI Feedback System

- **Real-time grammar corrections** appear after each message — what was wrong and why
- **Vocabulary suggestions:** Better or more natural word choices offered
- **Pronunciation feedback** in call/voice modes
- **Daily performance feedback:** End-of-session summary highlighting specific improvement areas
- Adapts difficulty: if learner excels → harder content; if struggling → simplifies
- Does NOT use a traditional points/badges system — instead continuously adapts content level
- Grammar feedback can feel overly pedantic at times (noted in user reviews)

---

## 4.3 Progress Tracking

- **Progress dashboard:** Total practice minutes, lessons completed, days active
- **Level progression system:** Visual indicator of how close you are to the next level
- **Vocabulary tracker:** Logs every new word encountered, tracks review strength and memory rate
- Color-coded topic wheel showing which areas need more practice
- Weekly charts for new terms learned
- No traditional XP or league system — purely skill-level based

---

## 4.4 Personalization

- Onboarding questions: current proficiency, goals (basics / fluency / speaking), preferred practice frequency
- AI adapts each session based on cumulative performance
- Content incorporates previously learned vocabulary into new conversations
- **5 English accent options:** Learners can switch between American, British, Australian, and other accents for listening variety
- Choose your AI tutor persona (different names/personalities available — e.g., "Emma")

---

## 4.5 Platforms & Social

- iOS, Android, Web
- **5-minute daily limit (free)** / Unlimited (Premium)
- **Business plan:** Manager dashboard with usage charts, quiz scores, time-on-task per employee; team performance tracking; manager can send coaching notes
- In-app translation feature for any word or phrase
- Real-time translation shown alongside conversations

---

# 5. BUSUU

**Category:** Structured Curriculum + Community Correction Platform  
**Model:** Freemium  
**Languages:** 14 (Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, English, Korean — varies)

---

## 5.1 Curriculum & Course Structure

- Courses created by linguists, mapped to **CEFR A1 through B2**
- 1,000+ lessons per major language
- **Topic-based units:** Travel, food, family, work, culture, emergency situations, social life
- Each unit includes: vocabulary, grammar explanation, dialogue, speaking exercise, writing exercise, review
- **Official McGraw-Hill partnership** for study materials integration
- Lessons are 10–15 minutes
- Grammar explanations included within lessons (not just implicit like Rosetta Stone)

---

## 5.2 Community Correction (Unique Feature)

- **Native speaker correction for writing and speaking exercises**
- Submit a written or spoken exercise → **native speakers of the target language review it** and provide corrections
- Learner in turn corrects exercises from learners of their native language (tandem system)
- Creates real human interaction in the learning loop
- Correction quality varies depending on community activity and response time (can be hours or days)
- Star rating system for corrections; best-rated corrections are highlighted
- Social element that no fully AI-driven app can replicate

---

## 5.3 Vocabulary Learning Tools

- Spaced repetition for vocabulary review
- Flashcard-style review mode
- Audio recordings from native speakers for each word
- Images paired with vocabulary
- Contextual sentences showing words in use
- Personal vocabulary notebook (save words for later review)

---

## 5.4 Conversation Practice (Speech Recognition)

- Built-in speech recognition for speaking exercises
- Record and playback own pronunciation
- Compare to native speaker audio
- Community speech feedback (native speakers can comment on pronunciation)
- Note: Speech recognition added recently; early reviews suggest it lags behind Rosetta Stone and ELSA in accuracy

---

## 5.5 Business / Busuu for Teams

- Corporate B2B platform with group management
- Track employee completion rates and progress
- Assign specific language courses to teams or individuals
- Certificate of completion (McGraw-Hill endorsed)

## 5.6 Gamification & Motivation

- Streaks, badges, learning certificates
- Weekly goals with XP tracking
- **Proficiency test at end of each unit** to verify comprehension
- Certificate of completion per unit and per language level
- "Study plan" feature: set a weekly study time goal; app plans lessons accordingly

## 5.7 Offline Access

- Review mode works offline with cached vocabulary

# 6. ROSETTA STONE

**Category:** Immersion-Based Language Learning (Pioneer)  
**Model:** Paid Subscription  
**Languages:** 25  
**Heritage:** Founded 1992; one of the longest-standing digital language learning products

---

## 6.1 Core Methodology: Total Immersion

- **No translation approach** by default — the target language is used from lesson 1, just as a child learns their first language
- Learns through **pictures, visual context, and repetition** rather than grammar tables or bilingual text
- Translations are hidden but can be enabled as an optional setting
- 20 learning units per language, each covering thematic topics (health, dining, travel, shopping, community)
- Each unit: 4 lessons. Each lesson has 4 sections:
  - **Core lesson (~30 min):** Primary instruction with picture-word-sound associations
  - **Pronunciation section (~10 min):** Speaking practice with TruAccent feedback
  - **Vocabulary section (~10 min):** Vocabulary reinforcement exercises
  - **Grammar section (~10 min):** Implicit grammar discovery through examples
- Designed to feel like moving to a new country and being immersed without a translator

---

## 6.2 TruAccent™ Speech Recognition

- Rosetta Stone's proprietary speech recognition technology, refined over decades
- Evaluates pronunciation in **real-time** and asks you to re-record if not meeting standard
- Compares to a database of native speaker speech patterns
- Widely regarded as one of the most rigorous speech recognition systems among language apps
- Used throughout lessons, not just in dedicated pronunciation sections
- Cannot be disabled — speaking is required for lesson completion

## 6.3 Stories Feature

# 7. PIMSLEUR

**Category:** Audio-First Language Learning  
**Model:** Subscription  
**Languages:** 50–51  
**Founded:** Based on method by Dr. Paul Pimsleur (1960s linguist)

---

## 7.1 Core Audio Method

- **30-minute daily audio lessons** are the foundational unit — everything is built around this
- Method based on Dr. Paul Pimsleur's research: language should be learned as it's naturally acquired, through listening and speaking, not reading/writing
- **Anticipation and recall technique:** Hear a word or phrase → get asked to recall it at intervals → correct answer revealed → reinforce
- **Spaced repetition for audio:** Vocabulary introduced and reintroduced at scientifically timed intervals within audio
- Authentic native speaker dialogues embedded in lessons
- Lessons simulate real conversations: you are a participant, not just a listener
- Progressive complexity: each lesson builds directly on the previous one
- Sessions must be done in order (not skippable like Babbel)

---

## 7.2 Supplementary Features (App)

- **Flashcard decks** for each lesson's vocabulary
- **Matching exercises** connecting target language to English
- **Reading activities** — available in scripts that use non-Latin alphabets (Japanese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Hebrew, Korean)
- **Review sessions** for previously covered material
- Core lessons remain audio; supplements are visual reinforcement only

---

## 7.3 Driving Mode

- **Driving Mode / Screen-Free Mode** specifically designed for use while driving
- Audio-only; no screen interaction required
- Works hands-free via Bluetooth speaker or car audio
- Designed so you can learn during a commute without ever looking at your phone
- Alexa compatible (some content accessible via Alexa voice command)

---

## 7.4 Language Breadth

- 50 languages including several uncommon options: Swahili, Tagalog, Ojibwe, Twi, Haitian Creole, Romanian, Hindi, Persian (Farsi), Swiss German, Lebanese Arabic, and many more
- Each language typically has 3–5 "levels" (Level 1 = ~30 lessons = ~15 hours)
- Premium subscription gives access to all lessons in a single chosen language
- All-Access gives all 50 languages
