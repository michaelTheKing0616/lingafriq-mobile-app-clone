Griot Adama's Recitation

A rhythmic exploration of ancestral lineage and the founding of the Mali Empire. Practice your Bambara phonetics while exploring the deep etymology of 'Mansa'.

01
Bambara

I ni ce, k'an b'a f'o ko Mansa Musa tun ye mɔgɔba ye.

English

Greetings, let us say that King Musa was a great person.

02
Active Segment

A ka mara ye sanu n'a sɔngɔ tɛ fɛn ye.

His reign was of gold and its value was beyond measure.

Sanu

bookmark

Noun. Meaning 'Gold'. In the context of the Mali Empire, it refers to both the currency and the symbolic divine wealth of the throne.
