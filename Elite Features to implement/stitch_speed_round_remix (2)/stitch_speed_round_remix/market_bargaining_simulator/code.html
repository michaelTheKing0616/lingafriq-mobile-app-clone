Griot's Insight

In Yoruba bargaining culture, it's not just about the money; it's a social dance. Always start with a greeting ("Ẹ kú iṣẹ́ o") and use polite honorifics. A smile and a compliment about the goods can often lower the price faster than a firm demand.
