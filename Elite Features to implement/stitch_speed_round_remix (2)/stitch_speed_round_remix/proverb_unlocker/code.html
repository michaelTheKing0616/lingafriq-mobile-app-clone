Griot's Insight

In the traditional Yoruba worldview, time is a circle rather than a line. This proverb emphasizes the concept of Historical Reciprocity.

"If you don't know where you're coming from, you can't know where you're going."

By decoding this phrase, you acknowledge that ancestors were the architects of the present, just as you are the architect of the generations to come. Use this wisdom to anchor your decisions today.
